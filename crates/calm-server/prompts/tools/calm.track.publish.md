Planner-only: deliver an attached track. When `neige/track-<id>` is at the commit of a `done` attempt of this track, push that commit (never forced) to the checkout's upstream URL and open the PR against the upstream branch, or reuse the open PR of `neige/track-<id>`. The call waits and returns `{ok: true, op_id, pr_number, head_sha, branch, base, url}`; `head_sha` is the pushed commit and the PR's head. Then merge as your template says. Refusals are JSON-RPC `-32409` with a message starting `refused:`, and nothing runs: `publish-not-a-candidate` (a commit made after the last attempt is not verified: let a task produce it, or undo it), `publish-candidate-not-done` (only a done attempt's commit can be published), `publish-no-upstream` (the checkout's branch has no upstream remote) and `publish-needs-track-worktree` (managed tracks and tracks without their own worktree cannot be published). A publish that ran and did not land (a rejected push, a failed `gh`) is `-32409` starting `publish-failed:`. `idempotency_key` is your request key: the same key replays the first result, a failure included, so after fixing the cause call again with a new key; a key already used for another commit is refused (`publish-key-reused`). `title` and `body` are the PR's; an existing open PR keeps its own.
