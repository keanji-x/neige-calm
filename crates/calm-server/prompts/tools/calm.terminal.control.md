Select exactly one terminal_id or task_id (exact attempt_id). Claim, release, or detach your terminal control connection. Claim is refused on a codex task Worker (error data.refusal codex_task_worker_input). A human takeover revokes your previous control. Claim deliberately only when the user asked you to operate the terminal. Prefer calm.terminal.input claim=true on a scenario's first input and release=true on its last; use this tool for a claim or release without a write. For claim/release, observe=true adds a fresh text observation; wait_ms, wait_for, settle_ms, signal_events, repaint_ms, wait_text and wait_text_absent have calm.terminal.observe's meaning, defaults and validation and require observe=true; the baseline is the screen and signal seq when the action started. Claim and release rarely change the screen: use wait_for=elapsed or a change budget of at most 500 ms here. A release readback unchanged since your previous observation on this connection omits the text array and reports text_omitted; claim readbacks always include text. A failed readback preserves the control receipt and reports observation unavailable. Claim and release receipts carry summary, a digest of the readback's wait and control facts (summary.screen: whether the revision moved since your previous observation; summary.wait: the wait outcome): read it first, never as a verdict; the text block says the same in one line. Detach closes your client, leaving the Terminal card and program alive, without observation or summary.
