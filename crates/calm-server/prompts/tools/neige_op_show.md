Planner/Worker view, served as `neige op show <op_id>`: the recorded outcome of forge action `op_id` from any card of this Track, in the forge-action envelope a plugin forge action's call and its replay return (not the outer result of a publish or a delivery). `status` is `pending` (the pending receipt), `succeeded` (`result`), or `failed`/`stuck` (isError, with `outcome`, `last_error` or `reason`, and `from_phase`). It never drives, waits on, or re-emits anything for the operation. An unknown op_id, another Track's operation and a non-forge operation are refused alike with -32404 `no operation <op_id> on this track`.
